HOW TO PLAY

Choose a tower at the bottom, then click an empty spot away from the enemy path. Start waves and stop the red things from reaching the end.

PING fires quickly. CRASH hits an area. NULL is slow but hits hard and slows enemies.

Everything about each stage lives in levels.js, so adding more levels later is intentionally easy.
