HOW TO PLAY

Pick a tower, click an empty spot away from the enemy path, then start a wave. Stop the red problem from reaching the exit.

PING fires fast. CRASH splashes. NULL slows and hits hard. ALONEWOOD covers a huge area but does tiny chip damage. BLUESCREEN charges for 7 seconds, then fires a piercing railgun laser.

The battlefield now scales to whatever space your browser has, so the title, arena, and controls should stay on-screen together.

Built-in stages are in levels.js. The Level Designer can make a quick path and test it without editing code.
