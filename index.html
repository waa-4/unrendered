HOW TO PLAY

Place at least 2 units, then press READY! once. Every later wave starts by itself after a 3-second break.

Use the arrows beside the unit bar to change pages. Click a placed unit to inspect it or sell it for 70% of its price.

PING fires quickly. CRASH has splash damage. NULL slows enemies. ALONEWOOD has huge range and low damage. BLUESCREEN fires a piercing railgun every 7 seconds.

INFICOOKIE is cheap rapid fire. BLEEDBOT causes damage over time. PUMPKILLER is a short-range heavy hitter. LOBBER NINJA throws long-range splash shots.

The battlefield stays at a 960×600 ratio and scales without stretching.

Difficulty mostly changes enemy HP. Higher difficulties also increase speed a little. Nightmare rolls its HP and speed multipliers when the level starts.

Endless keeps one map for the whole run and pulls waves from World 1. Later level waves enter the pool as the run gets longer.
