HOW TO PLAY

Pick a tower and place at least 2 units. READY! unlocks after the second unit is placed. Press it once; later waves start automatically after a 3-second break.

PING fires fast. CRASH splashes. NULL slows and hits hard. ALONEWOOD covers a huge area but does tiny chip damage. BLUESCREEN charges for 7 seconds, then fires a piercing railgun laser.

The battlefield keeps a 960×600 aspect ratio and scales to fit the browser without stretching.

Worlds and levels are stored in levels.js. The Level Designer can create and test simple custom levels.

Difficulty changes enemy HP and speed. Easy is 50%, Normal 100%, Hard 150%, Insane 200%, Ultra 300%, and Nightmare rolls between 400% and 575% at the start of every level.

Endless picks a random built-in level, moves to another random level after each clear, and keeps going until you lose. It uses your last selected difficulty.
