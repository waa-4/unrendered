HOW TO PLAY

Place at least 2 units, then press READY! once. Later waves start by themselves after a short break.

Tap a placed unit to inspect, upgrade it during the run, or sell it. Fun Mode disables selling.

World 2 has two paths and two bases. If either base reaches 0 HP, the level ends.

Completing levels earns Points. Points buy permanent upgrades from the home screen, including base health.

Endless keeps one map and pulls random waves from unlocked level pools. Duplicate units get more expensive there.

Use pause and speed controls above the battlefield. Settings can reduce shake, flashing, particles and sound.
